Maxim is developed as a falsification-oriented research project: features earn their
place through pre-registered experiments, and results that don't hold up are marked
rather than quietly removed. This page is the readable front door to that lab notebook.

The status on each entry is load-bearing, and uses the verdicts from the notebook's own
status audit:

- **Earned** — the run met its stated gates. Scope is still whatever the entry says it is.
- **Partial** — it ran and some gates failed. Exp 57 is the deliberate example: pooling
  passed its primary scaling gate and failed its total-experience gate, so it ships as a
  named partial rather than rounded up to a win.
- **Null** — it ran and the answer was no. A null is a result, not a failure to report.
- **Proof of concept**, **infrastructure**, **diagnostic** — the pipeline worked, the tooling
  shipped, or a cause was traced. None of these is a behavioral claim.
- **Reference** — a measurement or benchmark with no pass/fail gate.
- **Exploratory** — the instrument ran but the campaign was never frozen, so the numbers are
  not a result.
- **Pre-registered** — the design is committed; no data exists yet. **Never run** — designed,
  and then not run.
- **Superseded** and **withdrawn** — replaced by a later entry, or a conclusion taken back.
  Exp 48 is the example: re-baselined onto a corrected apparatus, then superseded by Exp 52.

Each title links to the full record in the
[pymaxim repo](https://github.com/dennys246/Maxim/tree/main/docs/experiments) — hypotheses,
methodology, raw JSON, and copy-paste reproduction commands pinned to a git hash. Where a
walkthrough on this site discusses the experiment, the entry also links to that section.
If this page and the linked record ever disagree, the record wins.

## A note on reading results

Sample sizes are often small (N in the single or low double digits), and the recurring
1.0 finding is sobering: a strong LLM's prior frequently dominates whatever the carried
substrate contributes. Treat each entry as one data point with its stated scope, not a
settled conclusion.
